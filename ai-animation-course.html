About This Course

Learn how to create animated videos and stories using artificial intelligence. This course is designed to guide you through the creative process, from developing your ideas and designing characters to creating scenes, generating animation, and assembling your finished work.

The course is currently being developed with practical demonstrations and creative workflows. Follow the YouTube channel for updates on its release and availability.

What You'll Explore

  • Developing ideas and visual concepts for animation
  • Designing characters and maintaining consistency
  • Creating environments and animated scenes
  • Generating movement and bringing scenes to life
  • Editing, sound, and completing animated projects
