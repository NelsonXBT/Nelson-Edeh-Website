About This Course

Turn your creative ideas into compelling films using artificial intelligence. This masterclass takes you through the filmmaking process, from developing an idea and building a story to designing characters, directing scenes, editing, and releasing your film.

The course is being developed with practical demonstrations and real creative workflows. Full course access and lessons will be announced when production is complete.

What You'll Explore

  • Developing ideas and turning them into stories
  • Designing consistent AI characters and worlds
  • Planning cinematic shots and camera movements
  • Directing scenes and producing AI-powered films
  • Editing, post-production, and releasing your film
