About Nelson Edeh Academy

Nelson Edeh Academy is an online learning platform offering practical courses, tutorials and educational resources designed to help individuals develop creative, technical and digital skills.

The academy is designed for individuals interested in learning new skills, improving their creative abilities and understanding how to use modern digital technologies and tools.

Our educational programmes may cover:

  • AI filmmaking.
  • AI-powered video production.
  • Animation and visual storytelling.
  • Digital content creation.
  • Creative technology.
  • Other related digital skills.

New courses and learning resources may be introduced over time.
