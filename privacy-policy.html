Privacy Policy

Nelson Edeh Academy respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and protect your information.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Introduction

Nelson Edeh Academy respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and protect personal information when you visit our website, register for an account, purchase a course or interact with our services.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have been informed about the privacy practices described in this policy.

Where consent is required by law, we will obtain it separately.

2. Who We Are

Nelson Edeh Academy is an online educational platform operated by Nelson Edeh.

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, the academy acts as the data controller for personal information collected and processed through its own services.

Contact: academy@nelsonedeh.com
Website: https://nelsonedeh.com
Location: Nigeria.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our platform, we may collect the following categories of information.

A. Personal and Account Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Account login information.
  • Account registration details.

B. Payment and Transaction Information

  • Course purchased.
  • Payment amount.
  • Transaction reference.
  • Payment date.
  • Transaction status.
  • Relevant payment confirmation information.

Payments are processed through third-party payment providers. We do not intend to collect or store your full payment card details or security codes on our own systems.

C. Learning Information

We may collect information about your use of the academy, including:

  • Courses enrolled in.
  • Lessons accessed.
  • Learning progress.
  • Course completion information.
  • Relevant learning activity.

D. Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type.
  • Device information.
  • Operating system.
  • Website activity.
  • Technical logs and diagnostic information.

The specific information collected depends on the technologies and services operating on our platform.

E. Communication Information

When you contact us, we may retain information contained in your messages, support requests and other communications.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage student accounts.
  • Process course purchases.
  • Confirm payments.
  • Provide access to purchased courses.
  • Track learning progress.
  • Deliver educational materials.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Investigate payment issues and refund requests.
  • Maintain the security of our platform.
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access.
  • Improve our website and learning experience.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory and accounting obligations.

We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with the purposes explained in this policy unless permitted by applicable law.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal information based on one or more of the following lawful grounds:

  • Contractual necessity: To create accounts, process purchases and provide purchased courses.
  • Legal obligations: To comply with applicable financial, tax, regulatory and other legal requirements.
  • Legitimate interests: To maintain security, prevent fraud, improve our services and manage our operations, where those interests do not override your rights.
  • Consent: Where consent is required, including certain optional marketing communications or non-essential tracking technologies.

You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share relevant information with trusted service providers where necessary to operate our platform.

These may include:

  • Payment processors such as Paystack.
  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Learning management and video hosting providers.
  • Email and communication service providers.
  • Security, analytics and technical service providers, where used.
  • Professional advisers and regulatory authorities where legally required.

These providers may process information on our behalf or independently under their own applicable privacy policies.

We aim to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place when personal information is shared.

7. Payment Security

Payments made through our website may be processed by Paystack or another payment provider.

Payment providers handle sensitive payment information through their own secure systems.

We may receive transaction references, payment status and other information needed to confirm purchases and provide course access.

Please review the relevant payment provider's privacy policy for details about its own data processing practices.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

This may include retaining certain records to:

  • Maintain student accounts and course access.
  • Preserve transaction and accounting records.
  • Resolve payment disputes.
  • Investigate fraudulent activities.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable retention requirements.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and notifying us if you suspect unauthorized access.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Request a portable copy of certain personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the email address provided below.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or its third-party service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain website functionality.
  • Support account authentication.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Understand website performance.
  • Improve the learning experience.

Where consent is required for non-essential cookies or tracking technologies, we will seek the appropriate consent.

You may also manage cookies through your browser settings.

12. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our courses and website may contain links to external websites, software and services.

These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

We encourage users to review their policies before providing personal information.

13. International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process or store information outside Nigeria.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer complies with applicable Nigerian data protection requirements and that appropriate safeguards are maintained.

14. Children's Privacy

Our platform is primarily intended for adults and individuals who are legally able to enter into the relevant purchasing arrangements.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection would violate applicable law.

Where a child is permitted to use an educational service under applicable law, the appropriate parental or guardian requirements must be observed.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal obligations or data processing practices.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

16. Contact and Complaints

For privacy-related questions, access requests, corrections or complaints, contact:

Nelson Edeh Academy
Email: academy@nelsonedeh.com
Website: https://nelsonedeh.com
Location: Nigeria.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where applicable.
