Refund Policy

We want students to feel confident when purchasing our educational products. This policy explains when refunds may be requested, how requests are reviewed, and how approved refunds are processed.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Our Refund Commitment

At Nelson Edeh Academy, we want students to feel confident when purchasing our educational products.

We understand that circumstances may change and that students may occasionally experience problems with their purchases.

This Refund Policy explains when refunds may be requested, how requests are reviewed and how approved refunds are processed.

2. Our 14-Day Refund Window

Students may submit a refund request within 14 calendar days of the original purchase date.

The 14-day period begins on the date payment is successfully completed.

However, submitting a request within 14 days does not automatically guarantee approval. Refund eligibility is assessed based on the circumstances of the request and the conditions below.

3. When You May Qualify for a Refund

A refund may be considered in the following circumstances:

  • You purchased the wrong course and contact us promptly.
  • You accidentally made a duplicate payment.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • You experienced a significant technical issue that prevented access to the purchased course and we were unable to resolve it.
  • The course delivered is materially different from what was advertised.
  • You have a valid reason for requesting a refund within the 14-day period and have not substantially consumed the course.

Each request will be reviewed individually.

4. When Refunds May Not Be Granted

A refund may be declined in circumstances including:

  • The 14-day refund period has expired.
  • You have substantially consumed the course content.
  • You have downloaded or copied substantial portions of the purchased materials.
  • You have shared or redistributed course materials without authorization.
  • You have completed most or all of the course.
  • You simply changed your mind after substantially using the educational materials.

These conditions do not override any mandatory consumer rights or remedies available under applicable law.

5. Duplicate and Failed Transactions

If you are charged more than once for the same purchase, please contact us with the relevant transaction details.

Where a payment is debited but the transaction is unsuccessful or course access is not provided, we will investigate the transaction.

If a duplicate or erroneous payment is confirmed, we will arrange an appropriate refund.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, send an email to:

academy@nelsonedeh.com

Use the subject line:

Refund Request – [Course Name]

Include the following information:

  1. Your full name.
  2. Email address used during purchase.
  3. Name of the course purchased.
  4. Date of purchase.
  5. Payment transaction reference or receipt.
  6. Reason for requesting a refund.

Please provide accurate information to help us review your request.

7. Refund Review and Processing

We will review refund requests and may contact you for additional information where necessary.

We aim to respond to refund requests within 3–5 business days.

If a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method, subject to the payment provider's available procedures.

The time required for the funds to appear in your account may depend on your bank or payment provider.

We will notify you once the refund has been initiated.

8. Course Access Following a Refund

Once a refund is approved and processed, access to the refunded course or digital product may be withdrawn.

You must stop using and must not redistribute any course materials for which you have received a refund.

9. Contact Us

For refund-related questions or assistance:

Email: academy@nelsonedeh.com
Website: nelsonedeh.com

We are committed to handling refund requests fairly, transparently and professionally.
